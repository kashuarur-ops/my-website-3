house rules

a few ground rules

this space only works if it stays kind and safe — please read before you comment or submit.

1.

be kind, always

no bullying, mocking, or making anyone feel small for sharing something real.

2.

protect each other's privacy

don't share anyone's full name, school, socials, or identifying details in a submission or comment.

3.

no hate, no harassment

zero tolerance for hate speech, discrimination, threats, or targeting another person.

4.

this isn't a place for explicit content

keep blog submissions free of sexual content as theirs readers of all ages on this platform.

5.

everything is reviewed first

every story is read by hand before it's posted, and you'll always be contacted before anything goes live.

6.

this isn't a substitute for real help

if you're going through something serious, please talk to a trusted adult, school counselor, or a helpline — this blog is support, not a crisis service.

if you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out to a trusted adult right away or contact a crisis line in your country — you deserve real support, and this website is not equipped to provide it.
